## About me

I am a Computer Science graduate from **Lebanese American University (LAU)**, currently pursuing an **MSc in ICT Engineering for Smart Societies** at **Politecnico di Torino, Italy**.

My research interests lie in **artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent decision-making**. Through academic and collaborative projects, I have explored algorithmic optimization, data processing, Internet of Things (IoT) systems, augmented reality, and software engineering. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent, data-driven solutions to real-world problems.

## Current academic work

**We DO — Weather/Climate Data Downloading and Elaborating Platform for the Built Environment**  
*Interdisciplinary team project · Politecnico di Torino · Ongoing*

Working with an interdisciplinary team on a proposed map-based platform for **historical and future climate data**. The project aims to support climate-data processing and validation, climate- and building-related performance indicators, interactive visualization, and weather-file generation for building analysis. My assigned work focuses on **data processing, quality checks, building-related KPIs, and system integration**.
